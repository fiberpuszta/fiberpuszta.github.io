PanzerHaus: The Crisis Management Program Linked to Lunar Diamond Mining Claims

A Czech-registered company offers crisis planning with opaque pricing and a multi-year onboarding process. Zero independent reviews exist. Its affiliate claims GDP from lunar diamond mining. A cautionary tale.

GK
Gergely K.
March 2026 · 4 min read

What PanzerHaus Claims to Be

PanzerHaus, operated by PanzerHaus s.r.o. (Czech company ID: 19776161, registered in Prague), offers "individualized crisis and security planning." The program includes property modifications, equipment installation, and ongoing operational readiness. Pricing is not published. The onboarding process takes up to three years.

What We Found

A systematic search across every major review platform, forum, and industry database returned zero independent reviews. Not negative reviews — zero reviews. For a company claiming to be an established international program, the complete absence of any external trace is itself a significant finding.

More concerning is the direct affiliation with an organization called Tri Super State (trisuperstate.com), which makes claims about lunar diamond mining and publishes GDP figures described as hundreds of times larger than the US economy.

Due Diligence Result
0 reviews
Zero independent reviews across any platform, despite claims of being an established international crisis management program. The affiliate organization claims lunar mining GDP exceeding the US economy by orders of magnitude.

The Lesson

The crisis preparedness space attracts both legitimate professionals and predatory operations that exploit fear. Basic due diligence applies: verify company registration, search for independent reviews, check affiliate organizations, and treat opaque pricing combined with multi-year commitments as a red flag. A legitimate crisis management provider will have references, published case studies, and a clear pricing structure.
